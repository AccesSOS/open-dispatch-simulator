# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep this a welcoming place for everyone —
including the deaf, hard-of-hearing, disabled, and emergency-services communities this project
exists to serve. Harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks are not tolerated.

One project-specific addition: **treat the subject matter with care.** Discussions here touch
real emergencies and real trauma. Do not post real call recordings, transcripts, or personal
data; do not make light of emergency callers or dispatchers.

Report conduct issues to hello@accessos.io. Maintainers may remove content, reject
contributions, or ban contributors for violations.
